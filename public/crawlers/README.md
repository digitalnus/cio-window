# Crawlers

Drop images of people or animals into this folder. Every so often, one of them is picked at random and crawls out of the window.

- Best results: PNG or WebP with a **transparent background** (a cut-out of the subject).
- Photos with a plain background also work, but the edges are softened with a mask.
- Supported: .png .webp .gif .jpg .jpeg .avif
- No restart needed in dev. Refresh the page after adding files.
- To test straight away, open the site with `?crawl=now` at the end of the URL.

Only use pictures you are happy to have shown (and committed to the repository).
